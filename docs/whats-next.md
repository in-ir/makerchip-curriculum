# What's Next

You just worked through the foundation of digital design: pure logic in Block 1, and memory and state in Block 2. That foundation is the hard part, and it's the part most courses rush. Everything from here is built out of what you already know.

Here's where this course is going.

## Coming soon: Tetris

Block 3 takes the registers and state machines you just learned and scales them up into **memory, register files, and a 2D grid** you can read, write, and search. The payoff is a complete, self-playing **Tetris**: falling pieces, collision detection, and line clears, all in TL-Verilog, all visualized in Makerchip.

If you've ever seen the *Nand to Tetris* course, this is that same spirit, with the added advantage that you *watch* your design run instead of just trusting that it does.

## In development: your own processor

Block 4 reveals the secret the whole course has been building toward: the decoder, the ALU, the counter, the register file, the memory, those were never separate topics. They're the parts of a **processor**. You'll wire them together into a working **RISC-V CPU** that runs a real program, one instruction at a time.
